Skin Disease Information

Learn about common skin conditions we can detect

Acne

Description: A common skin condition that occurs when hair follicles become clogged with oil and dead skin cells.

Symptoms: Whiteheads, blackheads, pimples, nodules, or cysts

Common Causes: Hormonal changes, bacteria, excess oil production

Treatment: Topical medications, antibiotics, lifestyle changes

Eczema (Atopic Dermatitis)

Description: A chronic condition that makes skin red, inflamed, and itchy.

Symptoms: Dry, itchy skin, redness, inflammation, rough patches

Common Causes: Genetic factors, environmental triggers, immune system

Treatment: Moisturizers, corticosteroid creams, avoiding triggers

Psoriasis

Description: An autoimmune condition causing rapid skin cell buildup resulting in scaling.

Symptoms: Red patches with silvery scales, dry cracked skin, itching

Common Causes: Immune system malfunction, genetic predisposition

Treatment: Topical treatments, phototherapy, systemic medications

Melanoma

Description: The most serious type of skin cancer developing in melanocytes.

Symptoms: New unusual moles, changes in existing moles, irregular borders

Common Causes: UV exposure, genetic factors, fair skin

Treatment: Surgical removal, immunotherapy, targeted therapy - SEEK IMMEDIATE MEDICAL ATTENTION

Contact Dermatitis

Description: Skin inflammation caused by contact with certain substances.

Symptoms: Redness, itching, burning, blisters

Common Causes: Allergens, irritants, chemicals

Treatment: Avoiding triggers, topical steroids, antihistamines

Rosacea

Description: A chronic condition causing facial redness and visible blood vessels.

Symptoms: Facial redness, swollen bumps, visible blood vessels, eye problems

Common Causes: Genetic factors, environmental triggers, abnormal blood vessels

Treatment: Medications, laser therapy, lifestyle modifications
