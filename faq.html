Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about DermAI

What is DermAI?

DermAI is an advanced AI-powered platform that uses deep learning technology to analyze skin conditions from uploaded images. It provides instant preliminary assessments to help users identify potential skin issues early. However, it should not replace professional medical diagnosis.

Is DermAI free to use?

Yes, DermAI offers free skin disease detection services. All core features including image analysis, history tracking, and comparison tools are available at no cost. Our mission is to make skin health screening accessible to everyone.

Can DermAI replace a dermatologist?

No. DermAI is a screening tool designed to provide preliminary information only. It cannot replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult with a qualified dermatologist or healthcare provider for proper evaluation and treatment of skin conditions.

How accurate is the AI detection?

Our AI model has been trained on thousands of dermatological images and achieves an accuracy rate of approximately 98.5% in controlled testing environments. However, accuracy can vary based on image quality, lighting, and the specific condition being analyzed. Results should always be verified by a medical professional.

What skin conditions can DermAI detect?

DermAI can identify various common skin conditions including acne, eczema, psoriasis, melanoma, dermatitis, rosacea, and several others. The system is continuously being updated with new conditions as our training data expands.

How should I take photos for best results?

For optimal results: Use natural daylight, keep the camera steady and focused, capture the affected area clearly without shadows, fill the frame with the skin area, and avoid using flash. See our Photography Guidelines page for detailed instructions.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes. All image analysis happens directly in your browser using client-side processing. Your images never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to save them. We do not store, transmit, or share your personal health data with third parties.

Do you store my uploaded images?

Images are only stored locally in your browser's storage if you enable the "Save History" feature in settings. We never upload images to our servers. You have full control over your data and can delete it anytime from the settings page.

What browsers are supported?

DermAI works best on modern browsers including Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+, and Edge 90+. For optimal performance and all features, we recommend using the latest version of Chrome or Firefox.

Can I use DermAI offline?

Yes! DermAI is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that can work offline once you've visited the site. The AI model and core functionality are cached in your browser, allowing you to analyze images even without an internet connection.

What image formats are accepted?

We accept JPEG, PNG, and JPG image formats. Maximum file size is 5MB. Images are automatically processed and optimized for analysis.

Why is the analysis taking so long?

Analysis typically takes 2-5 seconds. Longer processing times may occur due to: large image files, slow device performance, first-time model loading, or browser resource constraints. Try refreshing the page or using a smaller image.

How often should I check my skin?

Dermatologists recommend performing self-examinations monthly. Use DermAI to track changes over time with the comparison feature. However, if you notice any sudden changes, growth, or concerning symptoms, consult a healthcare provider immediately.
